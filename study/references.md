## Source records and attribution

The public archive and the researchers who contributed the run records are the source of all original classifier evaluations. Each catalog record retains the public run ID and flow ID, which lead to its original contributor and workflow. Numeric summaries and anonymized labels in the future-participant packet are transformations made in this study. The public archive specifies CC-BY for empirical data and metadata. No public license for private participant answers is claimed. [OpenML terms](https://www.openml.org/terms)

The seven task pages are [kr-vs-kp](https://www.openml.org/t/3), [letter](https://www.openml.org/t/6), [mfeat-factors](https://www.openml.org/t/12), [cmc](https://www.openml.org/t/23), [optdigits](https://www.openml.org/t/28), [credit-g](https://www.openml.org/t/31) and [diabetes](https://www.openml.org/t/37). Historical metrics were downloaded programmatically. Response digests for the first 384 evaluations per task and source metric are in api_sources.csv. The example with identical unrounded accuracy and kappa is credited to [run 256](https://www.openml.org/r/256) and [run 578244](https://www.openml.org/r/578244). AUC reaggregation uses [run 28](https://www.openml.org/r/28) and [run 73](https://www.openml.org/r/73), with original file sources and SHA-256 hashes in prediction_sources.csv. Public OpenML classification runs are used for the human packet, and the coordinator holds the individually attributable sources to keep assignments blinded.
