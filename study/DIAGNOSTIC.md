# Exploratory positive control
A preliminary scoring pass of the planned forced-choice trial found only four kappa-separable pairs. To assess numerical matching in cases where a discriminator is actually present, form a separate diagnostic from untouched runs, without changing the primary test. Partition the remaining runs within each task by integer accuracy, and pair runs having different tenths of kappa. Retain at most 16 pairs per task, ordering eligible pairs pseudorandomly (PCG64 seed 481129). Present each pair with both members independently designated the target, in separate prompts in fixed candidate order, always showing kappa. Score each checkpoint and the first-compatible rule. This enriches for separable cases and cannot estimate the frequency of useful kappa in the archive. No response to this extra trial had been collected when this plan was written.
